## Notes

Values from RobotEra's own STAR1 brochure PDF (robotera.com/upload, rendered and read): 171 cm, 63 kg, 55 DoF (7-DoF arms, 12-DoF XHAND1 hands, 3-DoF waist, 2-DoF neck), 400 Nm max joint torque, 25 rad/s, outdoor running 3.6 m/s, load capacity 20 kg, 275 TOPS (Nvidia Orin + Intel), tactile sensors, 3D cameras, mic array. Launched Aug 2024 (press). Aggregators claim STAR1 has been removed from the current robotera.com lineup in favor of L7 — not verified on a maker page, status left blank.
