## Notes

Maker repository and Yahboom's own store checked 2026-11-25: 12 DoF driven by 12 programmable metal bus servos (4.5 kg.cm), aluminium alloy frame, Raspberry Pi 5 as controller on raspios-bookworm + Docker + ROS 2 Humble, Python programming, IMU and servo-angle feedback, RVIZ and Gazebo simulation; the HD camera, MS200 LiDAR and voice module are listed for the S2 version only. Dimensions, weight, battery and runtime are not stated on the maker's current pages — a reseller table quotes 972 g, 246 x 145 x 195 mm and a 7.4 V 2500 mAh pack, which is not a maker figure and is not used here. battery capacity and runtime left empty.
