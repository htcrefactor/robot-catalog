## Notes

PNDbotics wiki (checked 2026-11-17) lists four editions: Adam Lite 60 kg and 25 DoF, Adam Standard 61 kg and 29 DoF, Adam SP 62 kg and 41 DoF, Adam Pro 63 kg and 43 DoF, all 1.67 m, walking up to 1.5 m/s on a 1,172 Wh pack. Recorded figures are the Standard, which has 6 DoF per leg, a 3-DoF waist and 7 DoF per arm. Payload, runtime and price are not published by the maker.
