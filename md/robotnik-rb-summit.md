## Notes

Robotnik product page: 731 x 614 x 720 mm, 65 kg, 50 kg payload, up to 2.5 m/s, up to 5 h, IP54 chassis, 5G router, 2D LiDARs (276°) and RGBD camera included, ROS 2 based. The maker's September 2024 datasheet instead gives 3 m/s and IP50 as standard with IP54 weatherproofing optional, so the product-page figures are kept and the datasheet ones recorded here. RB-SUMMIT+ adds a Kinova Gen3 Lite or Gen3 6/7 DoF arm with a 4 kg arm payload.
