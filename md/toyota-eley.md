## Notes

Maker figures: 478 x 450 x 939-1619 mm, with the body extending in height (the taller figure is recorded above), 50 kg, dual arms with a scapular axis, all joints quasi-direct-drive, omnidirectional wheeled base, 1 kg payload per arm, battery or cable power, two-finger hands. Total DoF, speed, runtime and price are not published. ELEY was first shown at the World Robot Summit 2025 Aichi and is described by Toyota as a research platform being prepared for production-site deployment, not as a product.
