## Notes

Specs are the 2011 'all-new ASIMO' per Honda (130x45x34 cm, 48 kg, 57 DoF, 9 km/h, 40 min). Earlier versions differ (2000: 120 cm/43 kg). Honda ended ASIMO development in 2018 (Nikkei report of 28 Jun 2018; Honda said humanoid research continues) -> Discontinued.
