# PAL Robotics StockBot

## Notes

Values from PAL Robotics' StockBot page and 2024 datasheet (checked 2026-11-25): 46 x 46 x 183 cm, two RAIN RFID readers with eight antennas (EPC Gen2 / ISO 18000-6C), 3D cameras for navigation and a 2D laser, autonomous dock charging, ERP/WMS integration through an API. Autonomy and charging figures differ between the two maker documents — the page says about 10 h of use and a 5 h recharge, the datasheet says 12 h of continuous use and a 4 h recharge — so 10 h is used and both are recorded here; capture height is 2.4 m in the datasheet and up to 4 m on the page. Weight, speed and payload are not published.
