## Notes

Maker page (Center of Advanced Systems and Technologies, University of Tehran) and the SURENA IV paper: 1.7 m, 68 kg, 43 DOF (7 per arm, 6 per hand, 6 per leg, 2 waist, 3 neck), continuous walking at 0.7 km/h (0.19 m/s), 200 Hz control loop on an FPGA board, ROS-based software, face/object detection, speech recognition and synthesis, online grip and imitation, no force/torque sensors (an inexpensive predictive foot sensor instead), i7 CPU with 32 GB RAM and a GTX 1060 GPU, 3D-printed SLA covers on a magnesium-alloy structure. Unveiled 2019-12-14. Unchanged from the earlier record: research platform, not sold, payload and battery not published.
