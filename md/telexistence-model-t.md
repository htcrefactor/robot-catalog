## Notes

TX release 2020-07-21: 22-DoF body and arms, 50 ms end-to-end video latency with H.265/HEVC, a combined vacuum and two-finger 'Andrea-Yamaura' end effector, trialled at Lawson and FamilyMart stores. TX's own About page states the Model-T was discontinued and its retail automation line moved to TX SCARA (announced November 2021).
