## Notes

Maker pages checked 2026-11-25: four feedback servos, one motor per leg driving shoulder and knee, backward-compatible OpenCat firmware (Petoi says it will open-source the Quaddle code before delivery), two ESP32-S3 boards (motion, and AI on Buddy/Scout) with a Raspberry Pi Zero socket, gyroscope and PIR on every model, gesture/light/IR distance sensors and an optional AI vision module on Scout, blocks/MicroPython/Python/C++/ROS programming. Dimensions, weight, battery capacity and speed are not published on the maker's pages and are left empty. Status Pre-order: the Kickstarter campaign is funded and the maker sells late pledges at US$154/189/229 against the MSRPs listed here.
