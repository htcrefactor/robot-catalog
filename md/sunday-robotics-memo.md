## Notes

Maker site: 170 cm resting at about 4 ft with arms reaching 7 ft (about 210 cm), 170 lb (77 kg), rolling base with passive stability and no legs, about 4 h runtime and 1 h recharge, speed limited by software to roughly half human pace (about 1 m/s), a single camera under the hat brim, compliant control, and DOF given only as a breakdown - arms 2 x 7, hands 2 x 4, torso 1, lower body 4, which sums to 27 (the total is our sum, not the maker's). Hands are IP67 and lower arms IP66. Not for sale: invite-only 2026 beta for Founding Families, and the maker says a hand-built unit costs about USD 20,000 today with retail pricing to be announced, so no price is entered. The earlier 6 kg payload figure is not maker-stated and was removed.
