## Notes

Foundation's own site (foundation.bot/phantom) presents a single 'Phantom' production humanoid - 1.8 m, 80 kg, 29 DoF, 1.7 m/s, 40 kg payload capacity, 160 N.m cycloid actuators - without separating MK-1 from MK-2, so nothing there can be attributed to the MK-1 specifically. The MK-1 figures in this record come from the HumanoidStore import; reporting on the MK-1 (Ukraine trials, BBC, TIME) gives about 20 kg payload, no waterproofing and insufficient battery life, and about 5 ft 9 in. Kept unconfirmed rather than restated as maker data; the MK-2 record carries the maker page's figures.
