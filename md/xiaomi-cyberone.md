Duplicate merged: 'Tieda' (铁大) is Xiaomi's own nickname for CyberOne, so the separate xiaomi-tieda record is kept here as a variant. Xiaomi product page: 177 cm, 52 kg, 21 DoF, 1.5 kg single-hand load, 3.6 km/h (1.0 m/s) max. The 2026-generation Tieda shown at WRC Aug 2026 (1.70 m, 66 kg, 66 DoF per press) has no maker spec page, so no second record is kept for it. Prototype, not for sale. | Maker product page: 177 cm, 52 kg, 21 DoF, 300 Nm peak joint torque, 96 Nm/kg torque density, 3.6 km/h (1.0 m/s), 1.5 kg held in one hand, Mi-Sense depth module with 99 % depth accuracy within 8 m, 85 environmental sounds and 45 emotions recognised, dual-microphone voice system, curved OLED face display.
