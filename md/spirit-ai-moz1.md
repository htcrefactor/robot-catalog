## Notes

Official Spirit AI (千寻智能) manual: Moz1 is a WHEELED humanoid (legs replaced by a 4-wheel omnidirectional mobile platform) - not bipedal. 26 joints = 4 base + 6 leg-waist + 7+7 arms + 2 neck; base Cartesian speed 500 mm/s; IP40; two-finger grippers standard; Intel D455 head cam, 2x D405 wrist cams, Livox MID-360 LiDAR. MozRobot SDK and Isaac/MuJoCo sim published. Launched June 2025. Height/weight not stated in pages opened (130 kg / 180 cm appear only on a retailer listing).
