## Notes

The maker's spec table (poster on TetraBOT's CDN, 2026-07-16) covers four variants that differ in size, weight and DoF, so variant-dependent fields are left empty: Lite 630x840x260 mm, 18 kg, 10 DoF (5 per arm), external power only; Pro 630x840x260 mm, 20 kg, 14 DoF (7 per arm), external power; Max 570x1470x550 mm folded / 570x1870x550 mm extended, 48 kg, 13 DoF (5 per arm, 1 waist, 2 base), about 1.5 h on battery; Ultra same size, 50 kg, 17 DoF (7 per arm, 1 waist, 2 base), about 1.5 h. Shared: 82 Nm peak shoulder torque, 3 kg hand payload, 740 mm arm span, 8-core CPU at 6 TOPS with optional Orin, 8-microphone array, 6 W speaker, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2, binocular head camera with optional wrist depth camera, base LiDAR on Max and Ultra.
