## Notes

The CSIRO page describes the capability without figures; the numbers come from the Data61 team's IROS 2018 paper (Bandyopadhyay et al.): 5.53 kg (5.87 kg with tether), 660 x 600 x 320 mm in operation and 370 x 210 x 250 mm packed, 3 DoF per leg (12), Kanetec KEP-4C magnets of 40 mm footprint, squeezes through a 230 mm gap. Figures are the operating envelope, not a standing height. Licensed to Nexxis for market.
