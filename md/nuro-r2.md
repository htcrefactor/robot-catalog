## Notes

Nuro R2 spec sheet: 2.74 m x 1.1 m x 1.88 m, max speed 25 mph (11.18 m/s), 31 kWh battery, gross weight 1,150 kg, payload 190 kg, 0.634 m3 cargo. Introduced Feb 2020 (first NHTSA exemption). Zero-occupant road vehicle, not a sidewalk robot. Succeeded by R3 (unveiled 2022) and Nuro has since pivoted to licensing its Nuro Driver software; maker does not state R2 is discontinued, so status left blank. release_year 2020 is from Nuro's Feb 2020 R2 announcement blog (seen in search index, not re-opened).
