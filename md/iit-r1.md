## Notes

IIT R1 technical specification (rev 1, 2019): 1.2 m tall with a torso adjustable from 1.15 m to 1.35 m, 51 kg with batteries, 400 x 350 mm footprint plus three castor wheels, two driving wheels with a 0.6 m/s software limit, two 8-DoF arms with a 1 kg target payload at 0.6 m, two 4-DoF hands, three hours of operation, YARP open-source APIs. The 28 DoF total (16 arms, 4 torso, 2 head, 2 wheels, 2 x 2 hand actuation) is the count IIT's own design paper publishes.
