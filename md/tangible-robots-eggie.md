## Notes

Maker (Tangible, San Francisco/Palo Alto; founder and CEO Bipasha Sen) revealed Eggie on 2025-11-18 as a wheeled, non-bipedal home robot with dexterous human-like hands, positioned on three pillars - dexterity, compliance and whole-body control - and trained from 'in the wild' data plus exoskeleton-captured tactile data. Its own site is a single landing page with no specifications, so height, weight, DOF, payload, speed, battery, runtime and price stay empty. Status is In development: no sale or pre-order, and BBC reporting in January 2026 showed tasks still performed under human teleoperation.
