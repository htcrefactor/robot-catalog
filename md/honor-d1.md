## Notes

Maker product page markets D1 (HONOR Robotics) on autonomous perception and navigation, an in-house high-dynamic motion system, terrain traversal, voice interaction and light strips/gestures; it publishes no height, mass, DoF, payload, battery or speed table. HONOR's own campaign material claims five track records (100 m 9.32 s, 400 m 39.45 s, 1500 m 2:30, half marathon 50:26, peak 14.5 m/s). Trade press gives 169 cm and 45 kg; those figures were not filled. A racing demonstrator rather than a product; no price.
