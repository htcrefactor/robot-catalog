## Notes

The catalog record is brand-level ('TARS'), so no single model's specs were written in. Maker (TARS Robotics, founded 2025-02-05, Beijing and Shanghai, AWE foundation model, SenseHub data system) sells two series sharing one upper body: the A Series industrial humanoid (wheeled or stationary base) and the T Series general-purpose bipedal humanoid. Maker press releases confirm the 2025-12-22 public debut demonstrating hand embroidery (needle threading and stitching with both hands at sub-millimetre precision) and the 2026-11-16 Aptiv agreement for 100 A-Series units across 8 process categories and 33 scenarios, with the first batch already running in a live Aptiv smart factory and mass production targeted for 2026. No maker datasheet is public and tars.ai blocks automated access, so height, weight, DOF, payload, speed and battery stay empty; secondary profiles give roughly 160-175 cm and 80 kg for the A Series and about 3 km/h, 3 h and 30-40 DOF for the T Series, which were not used. Status Commercial reflects the A-Series fleet in production; the T Series remains a pilot platform.
