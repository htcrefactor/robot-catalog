## Notes

Maker pages (IHMC Robotics humanoid design; Boardwalk Robotics Nadia): 29 joints with unusually large range of motion, hybrid actuation (Moog hydraulic Integrated Smart Actuators in legs and spine, electric actuators at hip/spine yaw and in the arms), carbon-fibre exoskeleton, VR teleoperation, IHMC whole-body control software; funded by ONR, ARL, NASA JSC and TARDEC. Height, mass, speed and runtime are not published by IHMC (190 cm / 100 kg / 3.5 km/h appear only in third-party catalogues and were not filled). No price.
