## Notes

Corrected against the maker's product page: official weight is 65 kg (HumanoidStore listed 58 kg). 42 total DOF (6 per leg, 7 per arm + 2 neck-related joints), 352 N.m max joint torque, 20 kg payload / 10 kg per arm in a test environment, 2 m/s, battery 15 Ah 0.72 kWh (=720 Wh) published as both, 275 TOPS compute, thigh 325 mm / calf 383 mm, arm 580 mm / hand 220 mm, PUDU DH11 hand with 12 tactile regions, 30 N palm-finger grip, 40 kg lifting load. Runtime (1.5 h in HumanoidStore) is NOT maker-published and was removed. Announced 2024-12-19; maker notes promotional features may not yet be available to customers.
