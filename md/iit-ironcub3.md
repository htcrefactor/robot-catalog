## Notes

Research platform, not a product: jet-powered flying humanoid built on iCub3 with four jet turbines (two on arms, two on a back jetpack); first liftoff ~50 cm reported in IIT press release 18 Jun 2025. Maker states iRonCub MK3 with jet engines weighs about 70 kg and turbines give >1000 N max thrust. Locomotion is legged (iCub walking base) plus aerial flight — no catalog category fits cleanly; consider out-of-scope for a mobile-robot catalog. Uses onboard IMU and RealSense camera for pose estimation.
