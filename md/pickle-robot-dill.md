## Notes

RENAME: 'Dill' was the robot's 2021 launch name; the maker now sells the 'Pickle Unload System' (truck unloading robot) and uses 'Dill' only for the 'Dill Autonomy Engine' software. Wheeled mobile base with industrial arm + suction gripper; drives itself deeper into the trailer. Maker states 400-1,500 cases/h, packages 6x6x6 in to 24x24x32 in up to 50 lb (22.7 kg -> payload_kg), setup <5 min dock to dock. 2021 launch press said peak 1,600-1,800 picks/h and 25 kg (KR30 version) — older figures.
