## Notes

Maker is Humanoid Robot (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., which operates the National and Local Co-built Humanoid Robot Innovation Center and the OpenLoong community (openloong.net). Its WRC 2026 exhibit entry describes L1 as a biped platform for general light-load work, built for legged-locomotion research and for validating embodied algorithms on 7-DoF humanoid arms, with low cost, high compatibility and modularity as the selling points; the entry publishes no numbers. Third-party catalogue figures (161 cm, 64 kg, 28 DoF, 2 kg arm payload, 3 m/s) were not filled.
