## Notes

CORRECTION: humanoid upper body on a holonomic (mecanum) wheeled base, not legged -> locomotion Wheeled. KIT data sheet (©2018): size 192 cm, working height 0-240 cm (telescopic torso), 160 kg without batteries, 27 DoF, 10 kg per arm (arm stretched out; 14 kg at average distance), platform speed 1 m/s. A later KIT paper states 28 DoF and a 40 kg battery pack (conflict on DoF; data sheet value used). Research platform of KIT H2T, not sold.
