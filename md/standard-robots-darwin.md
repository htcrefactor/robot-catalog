## Notes

Maker datasheet (DARWIN-01, distributed also by its Japanese partner): 680 x 640 x 1,736 mm standing (1,142 mm squatting, 859 mm folded), 200 kg body weight, 10 kg rated payload with a dexterous hand (5 kg with an industrial gripper), 23+ DOF including end effectors (chassis 3, waist 4, 7 per arm, head 2), 0-1,900 mm working range, 1.5 m/s maximum speed with omnidirectional drive, +/-10 mm and +/-1-degree docking accuracy, 48 V 30 Ah battery (=1,440 Wh, derived; the maker states only 48 V 30 Ah) for 6 h and 0.5 h charge to 80% DOD, 0-50 C, dual diagonal 2D LiDAR with optional 3D LiDAR plus depth camera and 360-degree aerial and floor obstacle avoidance, Wi-Fi and 5G, VR teleoperation for data collection. Launched 2025 and shown at WAIC 2025 with Mech-Mind picking software. Standard Robots' own product page lists only the OASIS mobile platforms, so the datasheet PDF is the maker spec source.
