## Notes

Tencent's own announcement of 20 November 2020: Jamoca is built on an externally supplied body, weighs about 70 kg, is 1 m long, 0.5 m wide and 0.75 m tall, walks, trots and jumps, and localises to within 1 cm with 10 ms replanning and 1 kHz torque control. One Tencent report gives 1.5 m for the length while the same announcement's figures give 1 m; the shorter figure is recorded. Tencent publishes no speed, payload, DoF (12 is usually assumed for this body), battery or IP figure; it is used for internal lab experiments only.
