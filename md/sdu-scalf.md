## Notes

Shandong University papers by the robot's own team: SCalf is 1.4 m long, 0.75 m wide and 200 kg overall, with a 200 kg load capacity, a 3-DoF leg (12 DoF) and a gasoline-engine hydraulic supply; the later SCalf-II is 1.1 x 0.45 x 1.1 m at 120 kg on a 19 MPa system. No maker product page exists, so the laboratory papers are the maker source.
