## Notes

Maker pages describe the picking robot and its vision and hand-eye system (line-laser binocular stereo vision on the body and in the head, two markers per hand, eye-to-hand servo tracking of palm and arm, hands with 9 DoF and 18 joints) but publish no dimensions, weight, total DoF, payload, speed, battery or price, so those fields are left empty. Vizum reported small-batch delivery of its bipedal humanoid in December 2024.
