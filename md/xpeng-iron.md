## Notes

XPENG's own releases: the first IRON (AI Day, 2024-11-06) was described with more than 60 joints and more than 200 degrees of freedom by the company's own count, and was trialled on the P7+ line in Guangzhou. The 2025-11-05 generation is quoted at 82 body DoF with 22-DoF hands, three Turing chips at 2250 TOPS effective (an earlier English release's 3000 TOPS does not match three chips), an all-solid-state battery, a bionic spine and flexible skin, and volume production targeted for the end of 2026. XPENG has not published height or weight for the current robot; the widely quoted 178 cm and 70 kg belong to the earlier IRON, so those fields are empty. [Updated 2026-11-26 from XPENG's AI Day 2026 release of 2026-11-25: the current generation is quoted at 76 DoF with a 21-DoF hand, more than 1,500 tactile sensing points over more than 80% of key body areas, three Turing chips (2,250 TOPS effective), an IRON OS whole-body stack and 360-degree perception; store-assistant training starts in Q1 2027 and public release is targeted for Q2 2027, with volume production by the end of 2026. XPENG's earlier 2025-11-05 release and its technology page give 82 body DoF and 22-DoF hands, so both pairs of figures exist in maker material; the November 2026 release is kept as the current value and the earlier one stays recorded here. The record's status stays 'In development': the schema's status list has no pre-production value and IRON is not on sale yet.]
