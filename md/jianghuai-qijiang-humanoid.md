## Notes

Maker exhibit entry (WRC 2026): Qijiang is about 1.8 m tall, about 60 kg, with 38 active whole-body DoF and intelligent decision-making, robust locomotion and dexterous control on a 'smart brain - agile cerebellum - strong limbs' stack, targeted at household chore, elderly-care, assembly and high-risk tasks. The Qijiang-2 prototype was unveiled on 8 Sep 2024 with the same 1.8 m / 60 kg / 38 DoF figures; a Qijiang-2M variant is 170 cm / 50 kg / 32 DoF / 200 TOPS. Speed, runtime, battery and price are not published.
