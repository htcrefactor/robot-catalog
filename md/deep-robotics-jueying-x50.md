## Notes

Maker product page (X50 / X50 Pro parameter table): 990 x 455 x 685 mm, 57.5 kg with battery, 50 kg continuous running load (200 kg standing limit), 2 h at 50 kg and 3.5 h at 20 kg, IP67, -20 to 55 C, 1.5 m/s working walk, 3 m/s sustained run, 2 km at 50 kg/3 m/s, 25 cm stairs, 35 deg slope, 4 lidars and one wide-angle camera. The 4.5 m/s figure is a laboratory extreme the maker says is not released for use, so it is not recorded. X50 Pro differs in height (705 mm), mass (60 kg) and I/O.
