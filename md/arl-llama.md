## Notes

Built at NASA JPL for the US Army Research Laboratory's Robotics Collaborative Technology Alliance; the JPL robot page gives 70 kg, 1 m length and 1.8 m/s. The JPL/ARL team's Field Robotics 2022 paper gives LLAMA 1.0 at 73 kg (tethered) and LLAMA 1.1 at 70 kg including an 8 kg 1.2 kWh battery, 12 DoF (3 per leg, single 5.25:1 quasi-direct-drive actuator design), 9 kg payload, ~35 min untethered, IP10 (environmental protection not a design goal), stereo cameras and 1-2 lidars. Runtime recorded as 35 min = 0.58 h.
