## Notes

Maker material (RobotCub leaflet and the iCub paper by IIT/Univ. of Genoa): 104 cm, 53 DoF (head 6, arms 7x2, hands 9x2, legs 6x2, waist 3), 25 kg without battery / 29 kg with battery (IIT facility documentation; weight field = 29 kg), 48 V on-board battery pack, ~4,000-point capacitive skin, YARP/ROS middleware, GPL/FDL open hardware and software. Earlier leaflets quote 22 kg. The 280,625 USD price was an aggregator conversion of a 250,000 EUR figure and was removed - IIT sells iCubs to labs by quotation.
