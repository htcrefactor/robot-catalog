## Notes

The project publishes no height, weight or whole-robot DoF: airec-waseda.jp sets out goals and tasks, and the JST progress reports describe subsystems (an 18-DoF rotary fluid-actuator body, a 4-finger fluid-driven hand with 14 DoF, 10-DoF electric multi-finger hands, 27.5 kg holding force in the worst posture). Figures such as about 68 kg have no official source and are not used. Locomotion is recorded as wheeled from the mobile humanoid prototype; the project does not state the form officially. Neither weight nor a whole-robot DoF count is published, so both stay empty.
