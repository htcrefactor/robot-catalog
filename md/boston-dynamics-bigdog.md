## Notes

The maker's legacy page describes BigDog without figures, so the numbers come from the Boston Dynamics team's own IFAC 2008 paper 'BigDog, the Rough-Terrain Quadruped Robot' (Raibert, Blankespoor, Nelson, Playter): ~109 kg, ~1 m tall, 1.1 m long, 0.3 m wide, 154 kg (340 lb) carried on flat ground, trot ~1.6 m/s, running trot ~2.0 m/s and briefly over 3.1 m/s while bounding (3.1 recorded as max speed), 4 hydraulic actuators per leg, ~50 sensors, water-cooled two-stroke engine.
