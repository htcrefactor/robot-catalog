## Notes

Values from Robotnik's 2024 RB-WATCHER datasheet and product page (checked 2026-11-25): 731 x 614 x 904 mm, 73 kg, 50 kg payload, up to 2.5 m/s, IP54, up to 5 h of continuous operation, 48 V 15 Ah LiFePO4 pack (720 Wh computed as V x Ah), four 500 W traction motors, ROS architecture, 3D SLAM and RTK GPS localisation.
