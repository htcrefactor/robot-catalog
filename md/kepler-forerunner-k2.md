## Notes

Maker press releases: Forerunner K2 launched at GITEX GLOBAL 2024 (Oct 2024) and described at WAIC 2025 as 175 cm, 75 kg, 52 DoF (22 hand DoF), 2.33 kWh battery for 8 h operation with 1 h charging, bimanual payload 25-30 kg and 15 kg per hand, 11-DoF tactile hands with 96 contact points per fingertip, 2 head DoF, 100 TOPS on-board. Pre-sales opened with three configurations (Bipedal Basic, Bipedal Developer, Wheeled Developer) from 248,000 CNY. The 34,000 USD price was an aggregator figure and was removed. No maker product-domain page was found, so official_url is empty.
