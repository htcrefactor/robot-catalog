## Notes

ITRI's own account (Industrial Technology and Information monthly, Aug 2026) describes two prototypes: a small one with ~5 kg payload and ~7.2 km/h (2 m/s) for culverts, and a large one with ~20 kg payload carrying lidar, thermal and gas sensors; both use 12 main joints. The record holds the large prototype's payload and the small prototype's speed. Fire test: 15 min at 2 m from the fire with a 100 C ceiling. Mass and dimensions are not published.
