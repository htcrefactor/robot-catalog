## Notes

Maker (checked 2026-11-17): 1.2 m, 35 kg, 25 powered joints (6 per leg, 5 per arm, 1 waist, 2 neck), 491 Wh battery, open CAD/simulation/firmware under CERN-OHL-S. Payload is rated per activity: 5 kg squat, 5 kg per arm sustained and 15 kg peak when curled or raised. The DIY kit is offered unassembled at $20,000; the maker says kits are shipping but sells them through a pre-order page.
