## Notes

Rename/correction: 'Sean' is the English name of Zeroth's N1 (小恩) home collaboration robot - NOT a 155 cm bipedal humanoid as aggregators claim. Maker-supplied IFA 2026 exhibitor copy: wheeled compact home robot, 30 kg, ~0.2 m2 footprint, 14 joints (telescopic left arm 4 joints, right cable-driven arm 7 joints, 3-finger gripper), 2-3 h active use, reach floor to countertop; detachable right arm. Launched at AWE 12 Mar 2026. No product page on zeroth0.com. Aggregator figures (155 cm, 45 kg, 16 DoF, 3 km/h) are not maker-stated. Re-checked 2026-11-17: zeroth0.com opened - its product menu now lists M1 / M1 EDU, W1 and Jupiter only (products/m1, /w1, /jupiter); there is still no N1/Sean product page. The maker-supplied IFA Berlin exhibitor profile remains the only maker document with figures (30 kg, ~0.2 m2 footprint, 14 joints, 2-3 h, floor-to-countertop reach).
