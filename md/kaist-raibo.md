## Notes

RAILAB's robots page describes RAIBO as a middle-sized quadruped for inspection, exploration and patrol with 12 powerful motors, and publishes no mass, dimensions or speed for RAIBO itself. Its successor RAIBO2 is 42 kg, up to 6 m/s and 8 h. Status kept as Discontinued on the strength of the successor being the platform RAION Robotics now takes to market.
