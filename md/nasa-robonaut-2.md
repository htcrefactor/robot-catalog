## Notes

NASA fact sheet (checked 2026-11-17): 330 lb (149.7 kg), 42 DoF (3 neck, 7 per arm, 12 per hand, 1 waist), up to 7 ft/s (2.13 m/s), shoulder width 2 ft 7 in, 350+ sensors, 38 PowerPC processors. The height of 3 ft 4 in is waist to head: R2 is a torso-only humanoid and the mobility legs added in 2014 are not covered by that figure. Each arm holds 20 lb (9.07 kg) in any pose in Earth gravity; the figure is a lift rating, not a payload spec.
