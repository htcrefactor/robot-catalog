## Notes

Automated reach truck family FM-X 12/17/25 iGo (dual seated/automated). Values filled for the top FM-X 25 iGo: 2,330 kg capacity, 2,830 x 1,302 mm, mast lowered 2,900 mm (standard mast), 4,310 kg; travel 2 m/s (7.2 km/h); lift up to 10,474 mm.
