## Notes

Unitree no longer carries a Laikago page (unitree.com/laikago returns 404, checked 2026-11-17). The figures recorded are the maker's own: the launch video on Unitree's channel gives 22 kg and 0.56 x 0.35 x 0.6 m standing, and Unitree's laikago_ros model has 12 revolute joints (3 per leg). Unitree never published a full Laikago datasheet - the 2020 page had an empty parameters section - so speed, payload, runtime and battery capacity stay empty rather than taking press figures (0.8 m/s and 7 kg appear in press coverage only). A later 2019 revision was listed at 24 kg.
