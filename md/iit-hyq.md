## Notes

Maker page (Dynamic Legged Systems): 1.0 x 0.5 x 0.98 m with legs stretched, 80 kg with external hydraulics and 100 kg with the onboard supply, 12 hydraulically actuated DoF, running up to 2 m/s, stereo camera and lidar, PC104 Pentium running real-time Linux. The 2011 design paper gives 70 kg / 91 kg for the same machine, so the maker's page figures are kept and the paper's are recorded here as the conflict.
