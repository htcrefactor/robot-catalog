## Notes

Maker: the base carries up to 380 kg and the front module up to 411 kg, with a 1,350 x 910 mm footprint; the arm adds 25 kg (UR20) or 35 kg (UR30); the total system is IP52, the arm and module IP54; REST and OPC-UA interfaces; ER-ABILITY software for programming and QR/barcode reading. Sold as ER-MAX 600-20 (UR20) and ER-MAX 600-30 (UR30). The maker states no travel speed or system weight on its own pages, and the 460 kg / 2 m/s that a reseller lists are not copied.
