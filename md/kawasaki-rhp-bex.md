## Notes

Kawasaki's own blog (interview, 2023) confirms RHP Bex as a four-legged Kaleido derivative first shown at iREX 2022 (March 2022), with knee wheels for rolling on flat ground (wheel-legged). Payload is a development TARGET of 100 kg (stated as target, filled as payload_kg with this caveat); demonstrated carrying a rider. Upper body modular (offered as open platform for partners). Still under development - status Research; no dims, weight, speed or price published. Kawasaki's separate CORLEO rideable quadruped (2025) is a different product.
