## Notes

No Gen B product page remains on anybotics.com (the current ANYmal page is Gen D). Maker documents read: ANYbotics' own ANYmal overview sheet (2017/11) - 30 kg, payload up to 10 kg, 80x60x40 cm lying / 80x60x70 cm standing, up to 1.0 m/s, 2-4 h autonomy, IP67, ROS - and the ANYmal article by ANYbotics' P. Fankhauser in HazardEx (Dec 2018) - 30 kg, 12 ANYdrive joints, IP67, 2-4 h. Runtime recorded as the top of the maker's 2-4 h range; the maker's URDF package is on the ANYbotics GitHub organisation.
