## Notes

Developed by University of Bonn (NimbRo/AIS) in cooperation with igus; open hardware released 2015, won the 2016 HARTING Open Source Prize. Not igus's current product: igus now sells the wheeled 'Iggy Rob' humanoid (2025), a different robot.
