## Notes

Maker blog (Kawasaki Robotics, 25 Mar 2026) covers Kaleido 9: leg structure redesigned to component ratings so the robot can step out faster and recover balance, developed since 2015 with commercial deployment in mind. Kawasaki's R&D magazine documents the programme's humanoid at 1.75 m, 84 kg and 32 DoF driven by electric actuators, with Hydro Servo Muscle electro-hydraulics for the legs. Version 9 itself (190 cm, 99 kg, 30 axes excluding hands, double the leg speed of v8) was reported from iREX 2025 press coverage rather than a spec sheet. The recorded 18 kg payload has no maker source and was cleared. Version 10, smaller, is in development.
