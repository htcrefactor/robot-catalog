## Notes

Maker product page: 101 kg, maximum component weight 1.5 kg, AMR basis ReBeLMove Pro, two ReBeL-6DOF-03 arms (664 mm reach, 2 kg per arm), suction pad plus ReBeL gripper, LiDAR and 3D cameras, up to 8 h per charge, igus Robot Control, ROS 2 interface, CE and VDE 5050 fleet approval. Maker press: 1.30-1.70 m depending on configuration, '16 DOF' quoted in the press release while the product page lists two 6-DoF arms (record keeps 12), 2.5 kg per arm, ReBeLMove Pro load 50 kg / payload 100 kg. Launch price 47,999 EUR (Iggy Rob Industrial, 2025); Iggy Rob Home from 55,000 EUR (Jun 2026). The 58,024 USD list figure was an aggregator conversion and was removed.
