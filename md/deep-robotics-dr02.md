## Notes

Maker product page (deeprobotics.cn/en/products/detail/5.html): standing size approx 1800 x 558 x 400 mm, 68 kg as DR02 and 78 kg as DR02 Pro (recorded the Pro, 31-DoF configuration: 6 per leg, 7 per arm, 3 waist, 2 head), 10 kg continuous payload, unloaded runtime ~3 h and loaded ~2.5 h with ~2.5 h charging, 68 cm arms, walking speed 1.5 m/s with a lab top speed of 4 m/s, 25 cm stairs, 40 cm step, 20 deg slope, IP66, -20 to 55 C, octa-core industrial processor with a 275 TOPS NVIDIA AGX Orin (AGX Thor 2,070 TOPS optional), LiDAR, 3 depth cameras, wide-angle camera, mic array and speaker, 72 V external power. Battery 20 Ah at 72 V = 1440 Wh per the maker datasheet PDF. The datasheet states a 175 cm standing height and 175/65 kg or 175/75 kg while the web page says ~1800 mm and 68/78 kg - both are maker documents, the web page values are recorded and the datasheet values noted here.
