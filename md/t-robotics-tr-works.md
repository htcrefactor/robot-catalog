## Notes

Maker product page lists TR-WORKS as its next-generation humanoid for semiconductor and display process logistics; the figures come from the company's statements at its first public showing (Automation World 2026, 2026-03-04): height 180 cm, weight 180 kg, payload 3 kg, 20 DOF (arms 14, body/legs 4, AMR 2), 55 cm up-down travel, +/-45-degree bending, +/-180-degree torso rotation, an AMR base with FMS integration, 3-finger and 2-finger interchangeable hands, teleoperation and VR teaching, imitation learning and RFM zero-shot recognition; the company plans to raise payload to 20-30 kg. Speed, battery and runtime are not published. Status is Prototype: it was exhibited and is being tested in the company's own lines and in a government project with a display maker and KAIST through 2030. A 2026-11-25 SK Securities note (Asia Economy) reports later figures — 140 cm, 131 kg, 30 kg payload and 8 h of continuous work on the AMR battery — and a first North American showing at CES 2027, with supply expected to start in 2027. Those are broker-report figures, not maker statements, so the fields keep the company's own Automation World 2026 values (180 cm, 180 kg, 3 kg) and only this note carries the difference; the 30 kg matches the company's stated plan to raise payload to 20-30 kg.
