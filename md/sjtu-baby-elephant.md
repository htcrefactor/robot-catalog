## Notes

From the Shanghai Jiao Tong University authors' own papers (ASME IDETC/CIE 2013 and the ASME Journal of Mechanisms and Robotics 2013): about 1 m tall, 1.2 m long, 0.5 m wide, about 130 kg, payload no less than 50 kg (a 2013 conference paper by the same group gives 60 kg), hydraulic actuation with three actuators per leg (12 in total, from the 2014 reaction-force paper) and a lithium battery in the belly; walks and trots outdoors by wireless remote control over 10 degree inclines. No speed, runtime or battery capacity is published.
