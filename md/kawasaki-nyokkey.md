## Notes

Maker news (22 Apr 2026): Nyokkey is a semi-humanoid social robot built for the same space as people, based on Kawasaki's industrial robot technology and aimed at guidance, reception and transport; at Kawasaki Robostage it now runs a retail picking station (tablet order, 3D camera over the shelf, autonomous move, pick and hand-over to the cashier). Earlier trials: pantry service at the Akashi plant (2022) and Fujita Health University Hospital elevator use. The maker publishes no datasheet; the 150 cm / 75 kg / 6 kg payload / 6 km/h figures come from 2022 press coverage and were not filled.
