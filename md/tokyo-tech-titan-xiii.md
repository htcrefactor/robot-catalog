## Notes

Figures are from the laboratory's own publications - ROBOMECH Journal 2016 (Kitano, Hirose, Horigome, Endo) and IROS 2013: standard posture 220 x 550 x 300 mm, 5.65 kg including the LiFePO4 battery (26.4 V, 1100 mAh), 12 DoF (3 per leg), maximum walking speed 1.38 m/s at COT 1.76, and a 5 kg walking payload (20 kg when statically supported). Published size differs between papers - 420 x 420 x 300 mm in the IROS 2013 table and 600 x 600 x 300 mm in the ROBOMECH 2013 table - and the ROBOMECH 2013 table lists 1.0 m/s and a 1 kg payload, so the 2016 journal values are recorded here. Battery Wh is not stated by the maker.
