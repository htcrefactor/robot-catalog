## Notes

Maker (NPO Android Technics, Magnitogorsk) describes FEDOR as an anthropomorphic rescue robot built under the FPV 'Rescuer' project, modified after handover to Roscosmos into Skybot F-850 for the Soyuz MS-14 flight (launched 2019-08-22, returned 2019-09-07). Nascent maker technical listings give 182 cm, 106 kg, 48 electromechanical drives, shoulder width 48 cm, about 1 h of continuous operation and walking speed about 4 km/h (1.11 m/s), Linux-based real-time OS; the ISS flight configuration is separately reported as 184 cm / 105 kg with 6 DOF per gripper and 10 kg per arm. CONFLICT recorded: press accounts of the 2019 flight give 160 kg for the ground model, so the weight depends on configuration; 106 kg is the maker listing figure. The legs were disabled in microgravity and the robot was teleoperated or ran predefined programs, so it is a research/demonstrator platform, never sold. No maker datasheet exists today - npo-at.com carries only news, so official_url points to the maker's FEDOR news item, and the 48 drives / 4 km/h / 1 h / 106 kg block is the most cited compilation of FEDOR listings (crediting Android Technics material), not a maker PDF. These figures are the least certain in this record.
