## Notes

Maker release 2023-05-16: 5 ft 7 in (170 cm), 155 lb (70.3 kg), maximum payload 55 lb (25 kg), maximum speed 3 mph (1.34 m/s), hands with 20 DOF total (the maker's own text says 20 'in total'; later material cites 20-21 per hand), Carbon control system with symbolic reasoning plus LLMs and RL, hydraulic actuation. LOCOMOTION CORRECTED: Generation 8 (release 2024-12-16) moved to a WHEELED base, with the maker stating customer feedback showed bipedal legs were too frail for a strong torso, so 'Legged' was changed to 'Wheeled'. Status stays Discontinued: Sanctuary's current site sells hardware-agnostic Physical AI deployed on commercially available robots and only describes 'a path to humanoids', and Phoenix no longer appears in its product material. No DOF total or battery is published, so those fields stay empty.
