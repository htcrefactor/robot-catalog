## Notes

Retrofit: Cyngn's DriveMod autonomy kit on a Motrec MT-160 tow tractor (vehicle built by Motrec, Canada). payload_kg = 500 lb deck load (226.8 kg); towing capacity is 12,000 lb (5,443 kg). Maker documents conflict on autonomous top speed: current product page and 2026 catalog say 4 mph (1.79 m/s, used); 2024 sheets said 2.7 mph and 4.5 mph, FAQ ~3 mph. Runtime: product page 'up to 12 h per charge' (used); 2024 sheet said 8 h. Dimensions 65 x 30 x 55 in; weight 1,250 ± 100 lb (567 kg) depending on options. Battery 48 V (Wh not stated). Sensors: 360° 3D LiDAR, 2D safety LiDAR (older sheet also lists RGB + ToF cameras). Price via EasyLaunch monthly subscription, no list price.
