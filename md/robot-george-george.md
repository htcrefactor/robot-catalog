## Notes

Maker site (Robot George a.s., Zlin, Czech Republic; 30+ staff, CEO Zdenek Huspenina, CTO Jiri Palka): GEORGE is an industrial humanoid with dual arms, quick-change wrists (4-finger dexterous hand, 2-jaw gripper, suction, hook) and one upper body mountable three ways - fixed pedestal, scissor lift, or walking legs - plus GeorgeOS with skills and fleet management, VR teleoperation and a mobile app. First series is not shipping: the site takes non-binding 'early access' pre-orders. No height, weight, payload, speed, DOF, battery or runtime figures are published, so all spec fields stay empty. Maker timeline on its own site: whole robot assembled June 2026, multi-task demos summer 2026.
