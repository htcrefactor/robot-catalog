## Notes

Values are for Pudu's current FlashBot (marketed as 'FlashBot New'/FlashBot Max, 2025 flyer): 538x534x1052 mm (LxWxH), 60 kg, 10 kg per layer (2-4 compartments; comparison sheet gives 20 kg max total), 0.5-1.2 m/s, 9 h run-time, 4 h charge, VSLAM+LiDAR SLAM, elevator integration. The original 2021 FlashBot (per Pudu datasheet seen only in search index: 539x515x1050 mm, 55 kg, 30 kg max, 12 h, up to 1.5 m/s) differs - catalog should decide which generation this record is. Payload left blank because per-layer vs total differs by generation.
