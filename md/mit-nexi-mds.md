## Notes

MIT News and the Media Lab's Personal Robots Group (checked 2026-11-17): Nexi is an MDS (mobile, dexterous, social) research robot with an expressive face, two arms with hands, cameras as eyes, a microphone array, a 3-D infrared camera and a laser rangefinder, on a balancing base that used supportive wheels in its early build. MIT gives no height or weight; an MIT-authored HRI paper puts the platform at about 48 in (122 cm) with 6-DoF arms and 2-DoF hands. No DoF total, payload, speed or production is documented. No further development or successor is documented, so the record is marked discontinued.
