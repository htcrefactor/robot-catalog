## Notes

The maker's site (shiyun-robot.com) did not respond when checked (HTTP 500) and no public datasheet exists, so the figures are Shiyun's own statements at the 2026-08-13 roll-off ceremony of its first batch, reported by Tianjin media: 174 cm tall, 55 kg, about 8 h standard runtime with 10-second hot-swap battery exchange for 7x24 operation, self-recovery within 5 s after a fall, four layers of safety including risk detection and touch-stop, terrain handling over grass, carpet, cobblestone, tile, glass, slopes and steps, and use in station guarding, industrial maintenance, special inspection, research, environmental survey and emergency response. First batch of 15 units, unit price above CNY 200,000, capacity target about 500 units by end-2026 and 1,000 next year. DOF, payload, speed, battery capacity and compute are not disclosed, so those fields stay empty. Shiyun (时耘科技, Tianjin, founded 2025-11) is a robot integrator focused on special-industry and high-risk scenarios, and this is its first own full-size humanoid.
