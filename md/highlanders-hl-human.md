## Notes

Maker press release (30 Jun 2025): 19 whole-body DoF including 4 per arm, 3 km/h (0.83 m/s) autonomous movement with 3D stereo camera and LiDAR, collision detection with automatic braking and distance-based slow-down, natural-language/VLA interface, 48 V power bus for add-on modules, SDK/API planned, optional 5-finger hand grips up to 3 kg (hand figure, not robot payload). Early Access from Q4 2025, mass production planned for 2026. No height, mass or runtime published.
