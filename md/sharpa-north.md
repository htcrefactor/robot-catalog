## Notes

Maker pages give only the 67-DOF total and the demonstrated capabilities: fully autonomous manipulation over four consecutive 8-hour days at CES 2026, ping-pong rallies with <0.02 s hand response, a 30+ step paper-windmill assembly, card dealing from a full deck with vision-language input, and camera use with ~2 mm finger positioning. The hand is the Sharpa Wave, 22 active DOF with sub-millimetre tactile resolution. Height, weight, payload, speed, battery, runtime, compute and price are not published, so those fields stay empty; the page is an enquiry form only ('Enquire about North').
