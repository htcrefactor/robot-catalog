## Notes

Official Hengbot Sirius user manual: 250 x 130 x 250 mm, 960 g, max load 300 g, max speed 0.4 m/s, active runtime 45-50 min (standby 100-120 min), Linux, C/C++/Python, 14 DoF (3 per leg + 2 head, per CN manual), Li-ion 7.4 V 2200 mAh (=~16.3 Wh, computed), camera up to 1080p60. Consumer toy-scale quadruped (~1 kg), launched June 2025 (Kickstarter); store currently waitlist.
