## Notes

Sony's own corporate history and press releases confirm the identity and status: SDR-4X (2002, 58 cm, 38 joints) and QRIO SDR-4XII (2003), which Sony's history page states explicitly was a prototype and never launched commercially, plus Sony's 2003-12-18 release announcing QRIO as the world's first running humanoid robot. Height 58 cm, width 27 cm, length 19 cm, mass 7 kg and 38 DOF (neck 4, waist 2, 5 per arm, 6 per leg, 5 independent fingers per hand) are the SDR-4XII figures published in the maker-side technical literature (Nagasaka, Sony, in Humanoid Robotics: A Reference). No maker page carries a full spec table today, and walking/running speed, payload, battery capacity and price are not published, so those fields stay empty. Project ended in 2006.
