## Notes

TRON 2 is a modular 'tri-form' robot (dual-arm / bipedal sole / wheeled legs), not a conventional humanoid. Height 987 mm (sole) / 1015 mm (wheeled). DoF = 2×7 arm + 2×5 leg + 2 head. payload_kg = 30 kg base load on flat ground (dual arms 10 kg). Max speed: sole 2–3 m/s, wheeled 3–5 m/s (sole lower bound used). Battery 46.8 V 9 Ah (≈421 Wh). Price from CNY 49,800 (launch news).
