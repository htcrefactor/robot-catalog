## Notes

Maker page (sber.ru/robocenter/green) gives 180 cm, 75 kg and 41 degrees of freedom, and lists the demonstrated skills (spatial orientation, simple tasks, self-correction, natural-language speech through GigaChat) and target work (kitting and packing, test tubes and biological samples). Additional maker-disclosed figures from Sber's own technical presentation, reported 2025-11-21: 0.9 m/s, 8 RGB cameras + 2 LiDAR, 48 V battery, 1 CPU + 2 GPU compute, Ethernet and CAN-FD onboard network, force-torque fingers and inertial modules; the maker page's speed tile does not render a value for us, so the 0.9 m/s comes from that disclosure report. Presented at AI Journey on 2025-11-20/21; Sber said Green would be integrated into business processes soon, hence Pilot. No DOF breakdown, payload or runtime is published.
