## Notes

Values are the maker's KOMPAÏ delivery Top module data sheet (FR and EN PDFs linked from kompairobotics.com; re-checked 2026-11-17): 620 x 510 x 1350 mm (LxWxH), 45 kg empty, up to 35 kg of goods, 2.0 m/s maximum clamped to 1 m/s (stored as 1.0), 8 h battery life with 4 h full recharge, 1.5 kWh 40 Ah battery, LIDAR + cameras, indoor only, CE marking. KOMPAÏ delivery is a RANGE: these are the Top module (medium cabinet) figures - Top module +, Transfer, Transfer +, Tractor and Container differ, and their data sheets could not be reached (the Top module + link on the range page returns 404). The Top module page text also says '6h non-stop', conflicting with the data sheet's 8 h; the data sheet value is kept. No IP rating published. Maker HQ Anglet, France.
