## Notes

Values from Pudu's BellaBot Pro product page (checked 2026-11-25): 570 x 550 x 1290 mm, 55 kg, 40 kg total load (10 kg per tray, four adjustable trays), cruise speed 0.2-1.2 m/s, run-time 11 h with no load, 4.5 h charging, 10.1-inch operation screen plus 18.5-inch advertising screen, VSLAM + marker + LiDAR SLAM navigation, 65 cm path clearance, two forward cameras, three RGBD sensors and one radar/LiDAR for front-chassis coverage; 25.6 Ah battery capacity is stated without a pack voltage, so no Wh figure is stored.
