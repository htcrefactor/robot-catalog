## Notes

Values from iRobot's own Create 3 documentation and 2022 launch release (checked 2026-11-25): maximum recommended payload 9 kg over the centre of gravity, true top speed 0.46 m/s with the safety override fully disabled (the robot limits itself to 0.306 m/s while cliff safety is active), 26 Wh 4S lithium-ion pack (nominal 14.4 V) shared with Roomba e/i series, differential drive with two wheels plus a front caster, sensors as listed, ROS 2 and the iRobot Education Python 3 SDK both stated by the maker. Weight and body dimensions are not given as text in the maker's documentation and are left empty. Price is the 2022 launch figure; no current maker price page was opened.
