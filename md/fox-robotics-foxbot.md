## Notes

Current model is FoxBot ATL Mk3 (autonomous trailer loader/unloader; Mk3 added loading, auto-adjusting fork tines, expanded sensors - maker blog Mar 2025). It is a retrofitted standard Class 1 three-wheel counterbalance electric forklift (manual driving still possible) - category is really autonomous forklift, not a typical AMR. Maker 1-pager: H 88 in (2235 mm), W 53 in (1346 mm), length with forks 110 in (2794 mm), 3,000 lb payload (1,361 kg), 16-18 h battery life (runtime_h=16; a Jan 2024 maker post said 18-20 h - conflict), operates down to -10 °C. Weight: Mark II user manual lists 7,189 lb excl. battery (excerpt only, older generation) - not filled. Top speed 2.35 m/s appears only on an aggregator - not filled.
