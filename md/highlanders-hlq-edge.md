## Notes

Maker press release (7 Sep 2026): payload up to 60 kg, walking height adjustable 250-500 mm (500 mm recorded as the top of the range), 12 DoF excluding the rear attachment, joints up to 330 Nm, 48 V battery for 3 h continuous, two depth cameras plus Hand Vision, Jetson Orin NX, 6-channel CAN/USB/RJ-45, Wi-Fi 6 and 5G, rear mount rail for the in-house arm. Mass, footprint and top speed are not published; the earlier HLQ Pro carried 20 kg for 4 h.
