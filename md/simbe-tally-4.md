## Notes

Values from Simbe's Tally 4.0 launch release (2026-01-12) and platform page (checked 2026-11-25): up to 12 h of operation with shorter charging cycles, ultra-high-resolution and specialty cameras, RealSense depth cameras, dual fisheye cameras for 360-degree capture, NVIDIA CUDA/TensorRT/DOCA Argus edge computing, optional Tally RFID (up to 100K+ EPCs per run), night scanning, and availability to Simbe customers from mid-2026. The maker states the robot runs at less than a third of human walking speed but publishes no numeric speed, and gives no weight, dimensions or payload, so those stay empty. Tally Spot and Tally RFID are described as separate offerings on the same platform.
