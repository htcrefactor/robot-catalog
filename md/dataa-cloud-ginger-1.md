## Notes

Maker product page (dataarobotics.com/zh/product-44): Cloud Ginger 1.0 / XR1 is 160 cm tall, 65 kg, with 34 intelligent flexible actuators (SCA) spanning neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hands, waist, knees and the omni-directional wheeled chassis, multiple 2D/3D cameras, LiDAR, ultrasonic sensors, IMU, force sensors and a microphone array, with perception and dialogue running on the HARIX cloud brain over 5G (the maker's own blog says the robot reported under 30 ms round-trip). Payload, speed, runtime, battery and price are not published on the page; the marketing blog notes the 1.0 hand carries about 0.5 kg against 5 kg on the 2.0. 2020 Red Star Design award.
