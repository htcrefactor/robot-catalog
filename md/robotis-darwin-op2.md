## Notes

Maker product page: 20 actuator modules (6 DOF leg x 2, 3 DOF arm x 2, 2 DOF neck), DYNAMIXEL MX-28T, CM-740 sub-controller, Intel Atom N2600 1.6 GHz dual-core PC with 4 GB DDR3 and 32 GB mSATA, default walking speed 24.0 cm/s at 0.25 s per step, 1,800 mAh LiPo good for about 30 minutes (hot-swappable with external power attached), 1 Mbps DYNAMIXEL bus, 3-axis gyro and accelerometer, two microphones. Height 454.5 mm and weight 3.0 kg are the maker's spec figures (identical frame to DARwIn-OP). Battery energy 20 Wh is derived (11.1 V x 1,800 mAh). Line discontinued.
