## Notes

Maker home page lists WORKMATE with 20+ sensors, hybrid walk + drive locomotion, 24/7 continuous operation and delivery in 2026; the launch release (Linz, 27 May 2026) adds the drone integrated in the head, the modular multi-head concept, the IONOSPHERE control/AI stack and pilots with industrial partners in Austria, Germany and Italy. No datasheet is published: height, mass, DoF, payload, runtime, battery and price stay empty (early interviews cited 175-177 cm, 70 kg and 30 kg payload for an earlier V1, which are not current figures).
