## Notes

Rainbow Robotics' current site no longer hosts a DRC-HUBO+ product page (only company news; the old rainbow.re.kr manual page is empty), so the maker-side source is the platform paper by the KAIST HUBO team that built it with Rainbow Robotics: 170 cm height, 80 kg mass, 32 DOF (7 per arm, 6 per leg, head and waist), wheel mode using wheels on both knees via a biped-to-wheel transition, LIDAR and head cameras, PODO software framework on Linux/Xenomai, exoskeletal aluminium structure, air-cooled high-ratio joints, FT sensors. Width 590 mm is from the maker-linked spec listing (robotsguide), the only figure available. The 31 DOF in this record was corrected to 32. The earlier 0.5 m/s max speed and 15 kg payload were aggregator figures with no maker value, so both were removed (robotsguide gives 1.5 km/h, not maker-confirmed). Won the 2015 DRC Finals; Rainbow sold units to universities as a research platform (e.g. 2021 order from a major US university).
