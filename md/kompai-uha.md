## Notes

Kompai page: 180 x 93 x 165 cm (LxWxH), 350 kg, up to 250 kg load, 0-15 km/h (= 4.17 m/s), 3 h autonomy, 3.4 kW, 48 V LiFePO4 (Ah not stated), 4 driving & steering wheels (omnidirectional), laser & GPS RTK navigation, CE certified; open firmware (kom4WD/komNAV) for R&D. Outdoor site-logistics courier (deployed at Université de Haute-Alsace, hence 'UHA'), not an indoor platform. Aggregator speed of 7.2 km/h conflicts with maker's 15 km/h.
