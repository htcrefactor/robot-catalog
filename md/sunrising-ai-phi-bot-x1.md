## Notes

Maker site (Sunrising AI / 光象科技, Tsinghua-incubated, founded 2025-04; released Phi-Bot X1 on 2026-06-10): 27 whole-body DOF with 1 kHz whole-joint control, 6 kg single-arm payload, 0-2.5 m working range, four-swerve-wheel omnidirectional chassis with active steering, crab and diagonal motion and in-place turning, 10 mm positioning and 0.05 mm end-effector repeatability, perception with 3 LiDAR, 3 RGBD (up to 5), 1 binocular camera and 8 ultrasonic radars, dual batteries with 1-minute hot-swap, quick-change end effectors.
