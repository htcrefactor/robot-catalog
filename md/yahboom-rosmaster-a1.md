## Notes

Maker's store and tutorial site (checked 2026-11-27): ROS 2 Humble, an Ackermann chassis with metal 520 encoder motors, a T-mini Plus LiDAR, a 3D depth camera in the Superior kit or a 2 MP pan-tilt camera in the Standard kit, an AI voice module, a Yahboom ROS expansion board, and a choice of Raspberry Pi 5 8 GB, Jetson Nano 4 GB or Jetson Orin Nano 8 GB as the main board, with Gazebo and RViz support. The dated price is the maker's own store listing for the Standard kit without a main board, which the store shows next to the same kit priced with each board.
