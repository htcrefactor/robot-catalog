## Notes

Maker spec sheet (robotics.hexagon.com/product): top speed 2.4 m/s, mass 60 kg, height 165 cm, 34 DoF, payload 15 kg short-term / 8 kg constant carry, 5 h autonomous work per charge with a 1-minute self-swap (record had 4 h, corrected to 5 h). Locomotion is wheel-legged: each leg ends in a foot-wheel that locks for stairs, so 'Wheeled' was corrected to 'Wheel-legged'. Launched 17 Jun 2025; pilots with Schaeffler, Pilatus and BMW (Leipzig). No price published.
