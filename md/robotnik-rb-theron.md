## Notes

Product page and May 2024 datasheet: 717 x 550 x 320 mm (418 mm with the lifting unit), 70 kg, 200 kg payload, 1.25 m/s, IP30, up to 8 h, differential drive with 2 x 250 W braked motors, 2D safety scanners and safety PLC included.
