## Notes

Maker statements conflict on DOF: Shanghai Electric's own LinkedIn post (2026-07-18) says 43 degrees of freedom, while the company's WAIC 2026 release (2026-07-24, via PR wire) says 41; 41 is used here as the later figure and 43 is recorded as the conflict (Jiefang Daily also reported 43, with 12 DOF per hand against 6 in the first generation). The maker confirms multimodal vision on head and torso and a dual-battery hot-swap system. Height 175 cm and weight 65 kg come from Jiefang Daily's July 2026 report, not from a maker release, and the 5 kg single-arm payload was quoted by Shanghai Electric staff at the June 2026 debut; 10 units were reported working on Shanghai Electric's own production lines (warehouse and electrical-cabinet tasks), hence Pilot. No speed, battery capacity, runtime, compute or price is published.
