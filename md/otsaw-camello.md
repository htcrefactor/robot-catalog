## Notes

OTSAW's own SEC prospectus (2025) confirms Camello as its last-mile delivery AMR and states the second generation, Camello+, launched January 2024 with up to 132 lb (60 kg) cargo - Camello+ values, not applied here. Re-checked 2026-11-17: otsaw.com returns 'Account Suspended'; the archived maker Camello page (Wayback, 2020 and 2023 snapshots) carries no specification table, only a brochure link; the archived copy of OTSAW's own Camello brochure is truncated at 1 MB and cannot be read, and the 2024 OTSAW brochure covers Camello+. No Camello-specific maker figure for height, weight or payload could be verified, so those fields stay empty (search-index copies: 20 kg load, 5 km/h, 100 L, ~6 h). Original Camello entered a one-year Punggol (Singapore) trial in Feb 2021. Status not stated by maker (likely superseded by Camello+).
