## Notes

OTSAW's own SEC prospectus (2025) confirms Camello as its last-mile delivery AMR and states the second generation, Camello+, launched January 2024 (delivery + security, indoor/outdoor, up to 132 lb cargo - Camello+ value, not applied here). otsaw.com currently returns 'Account Suspended', so the original Camello spec pages (search-index copies: 20 kg load, 5 km/h, 100 L, ~6 h) could not be opened and are left out. Original Camello entered a one-year Punggol (Singapore) trial in Feb 2021. Status not stated by maker (likely superseded by Camello+).
