## Notes

Maker page technical data (a family, so figures vary between versions): 0.40 x 0.30 x 0.25 m, 3 kg, 8 DoF, approx. 0.8 km/h (0.22 m/s), wired or battery power, series-elastic actuators, 16 position sensors plus inertial sensing. Whitebert is the 8-DoF original, Ebert the 12-DoF variant with an added shoulder joint; first shown in 2017. Germane reference: Graybert and Norbert were used on ESA's Mars testbed and the ISS Surface Avatar mission.
