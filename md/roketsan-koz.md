## Notes

Roketsan's own IDEF 2025 release lists METE as mounted 'on robot dog' (Robot Kopek Uzeri), which is the maker's only statement about KOZ; the page carries no weight, dimensions, speed, payload or endurance table. Turkish broadcast reporting (CNN Turk, 22 Jul 2025) adds up to 2.5 h of continuous mission time, four guided munitions and autonomous/remote modes, and TRT World reports a 45 deg climb and a TV plus thermal electro-optic suite; those are press figures and are not entered.
