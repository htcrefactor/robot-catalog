Official Noetix page re-checked 2026-11-17: BIPEDAL humanoid, 140 cm, 40 kg, max travel speed up to 3.5 m/s (as stated - flagged as unusually high for a 140 cm biped, but it is the maker's own figure), continuous walking payload 5 kg, knee torque 150 N·m, 10.6 Ah quick-swap battery (5 s swap), runtime up to 2 h; EDU edition with 67 TOPS compute board; SDK docs provided; modeled hands standard, dexterous hands optional.
