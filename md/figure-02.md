## Notes

Figure's own homepage at the time (Wayback snapshot, 31 Dec 2024) lists Figure 02 as 5'6" (1676 mm), 70 kg, 20 kg payload, 5 h runtime, 1.2 m/s, electric - the maker values stored here. The 6 Aug 2024 press release covers the 2.25 kWh torso battery, 6 onboard RGB cameras, 4th-gen hands with 16 DoF each (per hand, not total), onboard VLM and 3x compute vs Figure 01; tested at BMW Spartanburg. Figure began retiring F.02 after Figure 03 (Oct 2025) and announced fleet decommissioning on 30 Sep 2026 (a few units kept in storage) -> Discontinued. Total body DoF is not published by Figure (aggregators give 35/41) - left empty.
