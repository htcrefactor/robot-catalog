## Notes

NEURA 4NE1 Mini datasheet V1 (checked 2026-11-17): 132 cm, 36 kg, 25 DoF for the base unit excluding hands or end effectors (which may add up to 12 DoF each), 3 kg payload without stating the basis, 4.6 km/h, 2.5 h motion endurance, ROS 2 and Python SDK. Standard has no manipulation hands; Pro adds 12-DoF hands, a C++ SDK, digital twin and teleoperation.
