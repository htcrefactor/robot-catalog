## Notes

Maker repository and docs: open-source humanoid (hardware CERN-OHL-S, software GPL v3/MIT) with 20 Robstride quasi-direct-drive actuators (RS00 x2, RS02 x6, RS03 x8, RS04 x4) on a 48 V power + CAN bus, a 12 Ah NCM pack (=576 Wh derived), Onshape CAD, K-OS and K-Sim simulation stack. K-Scale Labs shut down in November 2025 and refunded pre-orders, so the design is no longer sold (status Discontinued). Height, mass and payload appear only in third-party catalogues (about 140 cm, 34 kg, 10 kg, 8,999 USD pre-order) and were not filled.
