## Notes

Only maker material is the 2026-11-04 launch announcement ('Introducing IGRIS-MAX. More power. Greater possibilities', ROBROS LinkedIn and YouTube launch film 'Built for More'), which states a carrying capacity of up to 30 kg enabled by increased shoulder joint torque, aimed at heavier industrial handling. ROBROS' website still lists only IGRIS-C under Humanoid, and no datasheet, dimensions, weight, DOF, battery, speed or price has been published, so those fields stay empty. The 30 kg is a maker claim shown as a chest-held load in a film; it has not been independently tested, and no duration or walking condition is given.
