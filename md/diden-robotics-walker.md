## Notes

Scope review (#16, 2026-11-17): kept in scope and moved Humanoid -> Mobile platform. Diden's own product page lists 'Diden Walker - a biped work platform with magnetic feet' as a separate product from 'Diden Humanoid', so it is not a humanoid but a legged mobile platform that carries task modules (manipulator, inspection equipment). It walks on its own: maker blog says the lower body went from hardware to stable walking in about three months, successful on its first physical test (GTC 2026 and MARS 2026 posts). Feet are electro-permanent-magnet (EPM) with a differential ankle joint. Exhibitor listing gives launch date Dec 1, 2026.
