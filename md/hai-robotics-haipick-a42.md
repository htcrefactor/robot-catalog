## Notes

Maker page (EN) confirms the HaiPick A42 multi-layer ACR series (four models, e.g. A42-G/M/L, fork variants A42-E4S/E6S): picks/stores cases up to 6 m (older material: 5.2 m), handles up to 9 cases at once (CN page: 6+1 trays), LiFePO4 battery, CE+NRTL. Per-model dimensions/weights (e.g. A42-G L1600 x W1000 x H4330 mm, 480 kg, 1.8 m/s; A42-L 550 kg, 1.7 m/s) come from a distributor (MMK Vietnam) - not filled because the series has no single value. Runtime >=6.5 h per full charge is from Hai's own Chinese product page (search excerpt, not opened). First commercial project March 2018 per a 2021 Hai product manual excerpt. Per-case load <=30 kg; total per trip up to 300 kg per Hai CN listing excerpt - not filled.
