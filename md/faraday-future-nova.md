## Notes

Maker pages (robotics.ff.com/us/nova and the 14 Oct 2026 launch page): 49.7 cm, 2.8 kg, 20 DoF (head 2 + arms 6 + legs 12), 0.1 m/s bipedal walking and 0.8 m/s on the detachable wheeled base, 2 cm obstacle height, 10 TOPS with 8 GB RAM and 32 GB storage, 2D LiDAR + ~4 MP wide-angle camera + 3D ToF + three-mic ring with sound-source localisation and voiceprint recognition, 240 x 296 2-inch face display, Wi-Fi 6 and BLE 5.4, open SDK with a ROS 2 workflow and a Sim2Real RL pipeline, four hours of runtime not stated by the maker so left empty. USD 2,490 device price (Skills Package USD 390 extra); Phase One invitations closed with units expected by end-November 2026 and open pre-orders shipping around January 2027.
