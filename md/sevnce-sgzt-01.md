## Notes

Maker launch release of 2 April 2024 (published with Sevnce as its source by OFweek, and repeated in the July 2025 review of the model line): whole-machine mass 149 kg, rated payload 200 kg, continuous working time 1.5 h, Ex IIB T4 Gb explosion protection, electro-hydrostatic drive. Sevnce's current site promotes the later X3 Stable and X5 and keeps no SGZT-01 page, so no dimension, DoF, IP rating or battery figure is filled; a distributor's page (100 kg / 30 kg / 1150 x 600 x 800 mm) is not a maker source and was not used. Superseded by X3 Stable and X5.
