## Notes

Ascento's own site (checked 2026-11-17) states 8 h battery life with fast charging and 24/7 availability for Guard, sold only as Robotics-as-a-Service. The maker's September 2023 launch factsheet, as reported at launch, gave 4.5 h and a walking speed of about 1.4 m/s with a system weight under 30 kg; those figures are press-sourced and the runtime conflict is recorded here rather than resolved. Ascento publishes no product page with a full specification table, so mass, dimensions and speed stay empty.
