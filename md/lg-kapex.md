## Notes

KIST (with LG Electronics and LG AI Research) states KAPEX is a full-size biped that keeps balance and moves while carrying more than 20 kg; 7-DoF leg joint structure, compound joints, in-house actuators up to 300 N·m, tactile multi-finger hand, VLM/RL-based learning, autonomous walking. Height, weight, total DoF, speed and battery are not published. KIST transferred the technology to LG Electronics in September 2025; field trials are planned through 2029-2030, so status is Research.
