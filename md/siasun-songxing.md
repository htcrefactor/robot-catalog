## Notes

No maker page or datasheet for a SIASUN model called 'Songxing' was found; SIASUN's current wheeled humanoids, published on its own site, are 松羿 (Songyi) in an Agile and a Power version. Maker-stated figures: Agile - omni-directional wheel base with a folding joint lift, working height 0.3-1.5 m, lateral translation and zero-radius turning, aimed at compact 3C and automotive lines; Power - column lift with a 900 mm stroke, 0-1.8 m working height, maximum dual-arm coordinated payload 15 kg, for high-frequency handling and palletizing. No height, weight, DOF, speed, battery or price was published for either, and no spec was written into this record. The humanoid.guide values (165 cm, 38 DOF, 85 kg, 3 km/h, 12 kg, 3 h, IP54, 140,000 USD) are marked 'not verified' with assumed values there and were not used; the record's identity itself should be confirmed with the maintainer - it may be a mistranslation of 松羿 (Songyi) or an earlier SIASUN humanoid.
