## Notes

Maker press kit and store checked 2026-11-28: 25 cm tall, 14 cm wide, under 800 g (store: 780 g), 15 DoF across legs, head and neck, Rockchip RK3566 with AI accelerator, 1 GB RAM and 32 GB storage, front camera, 8 x 8 ToF LiDAR, two IMUs, microphones and speaker, two NFC antennas, articulated grasping beak, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, removable NP-F550 2600 mAh battery with about 1 h runtime, 50 Hz onboard policy loop. Pre-orders opened 2026-08-27; the maker calls the open-source statement a software-only one (design files are not open hardware). Body length is not published, so length stays empty. Camera resolution, LiDAR range and radio versions are explicitly marked provisional by the maker and are not recorded. ROS support is not stated.
