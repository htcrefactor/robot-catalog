## Notes

Maker brochure (Jingchu Humanoid Robot Co., Wuhan, jingchurobot.cn): 165 cm, 70 kg, 18 DoF standard / 38 with dexterous hands (record keeps 18), 2 chassis DoF, 7-DoF arms, ~3 kg arm payload, 73 cm arm length, 2.3 m/s, 48 V 20 Ah (=960 Wh derived), about 8 h runtime, in-place rotation, 360-degree LiDAR + RGB-D, 5G, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, 4-microphone array, auto-charging for 24 h operation, self-diagnosis and recovery, hand remote and OTA. The brochure's compute line reads 'Intel Core i14', likely a typo. Deliveries of 40+ units across three models reported Nov 2026.
