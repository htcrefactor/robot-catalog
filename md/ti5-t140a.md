## Notes

Maker figures: 1.4 m, 45 kg, 1.26 m arm span, 42 DoF (head 1, waist 3, 7 per arm, 6 per leg), 30 joint motors, 3 kg per arm, 6-DoF hands, 1 m/s walking, 48 V 10 Ah battery (480 Wh derived as V x Ah), harmonic reducers, 16/18-bit encoders, RealSense D435i head camera, AGX Orin 64 GB.
