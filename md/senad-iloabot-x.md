## Notes

Maker product brochure (Oct 2026) and product page: size 852 mm (upper body) / 700 mm (chassis) x 800 mm x 1,850 mm with 0-2 m dynamic lift, net weight 250-300 kg (entered as the 275 kg midpoint of the maker's range), 22 DOF (8 per arm x 2, torso 1, head 2, chassis 3), payload 46 kg with both arms (clamp/grip: 23 kg rated and 35 kg momentary per arm; suction: 12 kg rated per arm), max forward reach 955 mm and max arm span 2,060 mm, 0-2 m/s in the plant with a 1.2 m/s safety limit, 48 V / 6 kWh battery (=6,000 Wh) with 12 h continuous operation, 2.5 h charge and hot-swappable packs, -10 to 55 C, vision + LiDAR + force + pose fusion with 8 vision sensors, 3 LiDAR, UWB, IMU and 2 six-axis force sensors, quick-change gripper and suction end effectors. First shown at WRC 2025 in August 2025.
