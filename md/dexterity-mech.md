## Notes

Wheeled dual-arm 'industrial superhumanoid': four-wheel-steerable rover base (moves, spins, strafes, climbs ramps) with two Kawasaki-built arms. Maker states 5.4 m armspan, 60 kg combined lift (Kawasaki release: 30 kg per arm), vertical reach >2.4 m, up to 16 cameras plus LiDAR, onboard 'supercomputer', operating 0-50 C, up to 1,500 m altitude (homepage says 5000 ft — minor conflict). Launched 17 Mar 2025; deployed for FedEx trailer loading and Sagawa Express validation.
