## Notes

Maker product page, launch release (27 Jan 2026) and the team's arXiv paper: 107 cm tall, 45 cm wide, 21 cm deep, 22.7 kg, 29 DoF including two active eyebrows, 6-DoF arms with 1-DoF grippers (fingertip grip limited to 12 N), 5-DoF legs, NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin 64GB with 1 TB SSD, ZED 2i stereo camera plus four ToF sensors, 9-axis IMU and a 4-mic array, 46.8 V pack at 5000 mAh (or 10,000 mAh extended) with 3-3.5 h runtime, 10-30 C operating range, indoor use, E-Stop, Fauna SDK, VR teleoperation with Meta Quest 3. Payload left empty because the maker's paper gives per-arm theoretical maxima by pose (3.7/1.75/0.7 kg forward raise, 11.2/5.0/1.1 kg bent elbow) rather than one rated figure. Price is not published.
