## Notes

MHI's technical review and news releases (checked 2026-11-17): 100 cm tall, 45 cm diameter, about 30 kg, 1 km/h on two driven wheels plus casters, 2 h driving and 2 h charging, 10 mm step clearance, face recognition for 2 owners and 8 others, about 10,000-word speech recognition. Joints are 3 in the neck, 4 per arm and 2 drive wheels, so 13 DoF in total (summed from the maker's breakdown). MHI rented rather than sold it and ended the line.
