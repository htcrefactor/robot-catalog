## Notes

Boston Dynamics 'LittleDog Robot 1.0 User Guide' (2006, A. Saunders): 340x180x143 mm, 3 kg, 12 actuated DoF (3 per leg), ~30 min on the 2.1 Ah 14.8 V LiPo, x86 266 MHz with 128 MB RAM and 802.11b, LittleDog API, Vicon motion capture for ground truth. Battery 31.1 Wh is derived from 2.1 Ah x 14.8 V (maker gives Ah, not Wh). The guide is hosted on CMU's BDI archive, not bostondynamics.com, and the maker site has no LittleDog page.
