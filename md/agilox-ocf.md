## Notes

OCF = Omnidirectional Counterbalance Forklift (autonomous forklift AMR with three omnidirectional drive units), launched 2021 (maker blog Sep 2021). Datasheet 02/2026 (OCF 1200 standard): 2,795 x 1,200 x 2,566 mm (older V03/2023 sheet: 2,784 mm length), ~3,600 kg, max payload 1,500 kg (750/1,200 kg on some fork variants), max lift 1,600 mm, station height 1,450 mm, 1.4 m/s. Battery LiFePO4, '3 min charging = 1 h operation' opportunity charging (Wh not stated). Certified CE, ISO 3691-4, UL 3100. No IP rating stated.
