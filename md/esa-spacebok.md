## Notes

ETH Robotic Systems Lab page: 22 kg, 2 DoF per leg (8), hip height 500 mm (recorded as height - it is the hip height, not an overall standing height), developed 2018-2021 and not space-graded. The ICRA 2019 paper gives 1 m/s walking and jumps to 1.05 m; ESA reports a 4 m jump potential in lunar gravity. Built with ZHAW under Marco Hutter and Hendrik Kolvenbach.
