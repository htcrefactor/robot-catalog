## What it covers

- `data/parts/*.json` — the source records, one file per category
- `data/robots.json` — the merged dataset the site reads (`meta` + `robots`)
- `data/robots.csv` — the same records, one row per robot, generated by `scripts/merge.py`

## Third-party data inside the catalog

Some values come from another open dataset and keep that dataset's attribution:

- **HumanoidStore open data** (https://humanoidstore.co/data, CC BY 4.0) — prices, sales status
  and public tender prices for humanoids. Records built on it carry
  `"data_source": "HumanoidStore open data (CC BY 4.0)"`; keep that credit when you reuse those rows.
- Everything else comes from the maker's own page, datasheet, manual or paper listed in that
  record's `sources`.

Manufacturer and product names are trademarks of their owners. This catalog is independent and
not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

## What this license does not cover

- The site's HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and the Robot Catalog name.
- Manufacturer names, logos, photos and datasheets, which stay with their owners.

## No warranty

Values are as the sources stated them on the dates checked. They can go stale, and a value the
maker does not publish is left empty rather than estimated. Check the maker's own page before a
purchase, a deployment or a safety decision. To correct a record, open an issue:
https://github.com/htcrefactor/robot-catalog/issues/new?template=correction.yml
